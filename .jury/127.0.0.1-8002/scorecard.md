**The idea, as the critic read it:** A heritage batik house from Paoman, Indramayu, selling authentic coastal batik and telling the craft story behind it.
**Critic overall: 6.3** (0.4 Design + 0.3 Usability + 0.2 Creativity + 0.1 Content, on the critic's strict scale)
**Jury estimate: about 7.4** (critic + 1.1, measured on two recent Sites of the Day)
**Band, from the estimate:** Site of the Day range (7.2 and up) — by the formula; caveat: the +1.1 gap was measured on two winners only, and is unmeasured on weaker sites (see `references/calibration.md`), so treat the band as optimistic.

## Fixes, most score first
1. [Usability/WPO] Fix throttled-mobile performance: home `/` mobile goto timed out at 60 s; content-page mobile LCP is 6.0–10.7 s (`summary.json`: index 5992, galeri 6156, belanja 9752, profile 10688 ms). The page is ~2.7 MB, images 2.6 MB, mostly hotlinked from pinimg/gstatic. Self-host the images, compress/resize them, add `width`/`height` to every `<img>` (also fixes the belanja CLS 0.319/0.164 and most of the 157 audit WARNs), and lazy-load below-fold images.
2. [Accessibility/Usability] Fix contrast in one token change: white on #1687d9 is 3.81:1 (needs 4.5:1) — it repeats on every page, 107 instances on belanja alone (`belanja_html` report `a11y`). Darken the blue toward #0b5fa5 and lighten the footer text #64748b on #0b1754 (3.48:1) toward #94a3b8.
3. [Usability] Make mobile navigation consistent and tappable: `galeri-m01.png` / `belanja-m01.png` show no hamburger while `index-m01.png` does; the 8 nav links are 18 px tall (`smallTargets`, all pages). Put the hamburger on every page and raise nav targets to at least 24 px (44 px ideal).
4. [Semantics] Give login/register an `<h1>` (their titles are `<h2>`: "Masuk ke Akun", "Buat Akun Baru"), add `<main>`, fix the nameless link (`.btn-pill > a[href="index.html"]`, login; `button[type="submit"] > a[href="index.html"]`, register), replace the clickable `<div>`s on `/` and `/index.html` with `<button>`/`<a>`, add alt text to the galeri image, and add canonical + OG/Twitter + sitemap (audit: 9 FAIL, 157 WARN).
5. [Usability] Stop the `/api/session-token` 401: it fires on every page and every pass (14 errors on most pages, 20 on profile, 31 on dashboard, `summary.json`). Guest pages should not call an endpoint that 401s — gate it behind login or return 200 with an empty session.
6. [Accessibility] Respect reduced motion: `reduced.hiddenText: 4` and `reduced.moving: true` on index/galeri/belanja (`summary.json`) — scroll-reveal text stays hidden and the marquee keeps moving with `prefers-reduced-motion` on. Show the text and stop the marquee.
7. [Design] Design a real 404 (`404/d-01-hero.png` is the raw Laravel "404 | Not Found"); fix the `home/d-04-scroll.png` card overlap ("Kain Panjang • 45 Hari" over ghost text); self-host the broken "Kain Batik" image on profile (pinimg URL blocked by ORB, `profile_html` report `failed`); replace or credit the watermarked "detik" statue photo (`profile-m01.png`).
8. [Usability] Fix register keyboard trap (`register_html` report `keyboard.stuck: true`) and give primary buttons a real hover treatment — "Daftar Sekarang" has no hover change and login's 4 of 7 targets are color-only (`hover.colorOnly`).
9. [Responsive] Stop the floating sparkle button covering content on mobile (`galeri-m01.png`, `belanja-m01.png`, `profile-m01.png`); fix the mobile brand lockup wrapping to two lines (`index-m01.png`, `profile-m01.png`).
10. [Content] Make the gallery count stable (badge reads 54 in `galeri-d01.png`, 30 in `index-d90a.png`) and replace hotlinked imagery with self-hosted files.
